## QUEST: Where They Went
**Genre:** Expedition | **Tone:** identity_erosion / numb
**Location:** Edge of Oblivion | **Faction:** OmniCorp Shipping

**Trigger:**
Hammond Osei walked out the airlock at 0400. No suit. No gear. No note. Just walked into the ice in their sleeping clothes. By the time anyone noticed, the footprints were filling with snow. The trail heads toward the bore shaft. Hammond has an eight-hour head start.

**Setup:**
The footprints are steady. Not stumbling. Not running. Walking with purpose. She looked at her name on the roster and it took a moment to recognize it. Following the trail in the vehicle, you can see where Hammond stopped. Once. To look up at the sky. Then kept walking. In this cold, unprotected, survival time is measured in hours. Hammond shouldn't be alive. The trail says otherwise.

A colonist died in Section B. They cleaned the room and assigned it to the next arrival.

**Objectives:**
1. Follow the trail before the storm buries it. The wind is picking up. You've maybe three hours.
2. Find Hammond. Alive or otherwise. Bring closure or bring them back.
3. Understand why. Hammond wasn't suicidal. Wasn't depressed. Was functional yesterday. Something happened between lights out and 0400.

**The Choice:**
If found alive: bring Hammond back against their will. They'll fight it. They say they've to keep going. Something is waiting.
OR
If found alive: go with them. See where the trail leads. Whatever Hammond is walking toward, it pulled hard enough to override every survival instinct. That kind of pull is worth understanding.

**Twist:**
Hammond is found. Alive. Standing at the edge of the bore shaft. Not cold. The air around them is warm. They're talking to something you can't see. They turn and say: 'It wants to meet you.'

**Dialogue During Quest:**
- Hammond: "I'm not who they think I am."
- Player: [Respond / Stay Silent / Leave]
- Hammond: "The night shift has rules. Follow them. Even the ones that sound crazy."

**Reward:** 13 thermal cores, a secret -- the kind that changes what you're willing to do
