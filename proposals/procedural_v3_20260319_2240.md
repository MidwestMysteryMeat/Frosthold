## LOCATION: Slagvale
**Planet:** Nerthus-9 | **Type:** quarantined medical wing
**Tone:** religious_fervor

**Approach:**
The approach is marked by cargo containers, empty, arranged in a semicircle like a barricade. Or a greeting.

**Interior:**
The signal was proof. The frequency was a hymn. The planet was the choir.

The main corridor runs deeper than the structure suggests from outside. The walls are covered in condensation that forms patterns.

A section of wall has been reinforced from the inside. As if to keep something in, not out.

**History Layers:**
- **Pre-colony (estimated 10,000+ years ago):** Bore samples from the walls contain organic structures arranged in mathematical patterns.
- **First survey (2566):** A handwritten note on the wall: 'Don't DRILL BELOW 200M.' The drill went to 340.
- **Current posting:** Used as overflow housing during the last emergency. Three colonists who stayed there requested transfers afterward.

**Environmental:**
- Temperature: -24C (hypothermia risk after 30 minutes without thermal gear)
- Atmosphere: contaminated below the third level (Endurance check every hour, difficulty: hard)
- Radiation: 12 mSv/hr in the lower level (safe for 3 hours, Medical check after)
- Visibility: 64% (a haze that has no identifiable source)
- Sound: a low-frequency vibration. Below hearing. Above feeling. The equipment detects it. The body knows it.

**Resources:**
- Thermal cores: low density (Mining check, difficulty: hard)
- Salvage: Mammona-grade hardware, serialized and tracked (Repair check, difficulty: normal)
- Crystal formations in the deeper chambers. They resonate when touched. (Survival check, handle with sealed gloves only)
- Water: condensation collection possible (Survival check, difficulty: easy)
- Data: hardcopy files in a locked cabinet. The lock is biometric. The biometric is for someone who isn't here. (Research check, difficulty: extreme)

**Territorial:**
- Iron Shadow Collective claims: classified research installation. Don't approach
- Veil Covenant interest: recruitment ground. Desperate people in abandoned places
- Blind Covenant: believe something important is buried here
- Colony opinion: "don't go there after dark." Unofficial but unanimous.

**Event History:**
- Day 10: A colonist reported hearing their own name spoken from an empty room. Audio logs confirm it.
- Day 56: Two members of a survey team returned. The team had three members.
- Day 110: A supply crate addressed to the site arrived. The crate was shipped before the colony was established.

**Potential Events:**
- If explored: discovery of pre-colony remains (triggers Investigation quest)
- If mined: thermal core extraction possible but structural collapse risk (d100 Mining check, difficulty: hard)
- If sealed: the seal holds for exactly seventeen days. Then it doesn't.

**Found Here:**
- a photograph of the colony taken from inside the site. The photograph predates the colony.
- three identical photographs of the same hallway taken decades apart. Nothing has changed. Nothing.
- A data pad: "Don't RESPOND TO THE VOICE IN CORRIDOR 7. IT KNOWS YOUR NAME. THAT Doesn't MEAN It's FRIENDLY."

**Location Secrets:**
- Scorch marks on the ceiling of the lowest room. The marks form letters in a script nobody recognizes. The letters were burned from below, not above.
- Gravity measurements inside the site are 0.2% lower than outside. The difference is too small to feel. The instruments feel it.
- Radio signals from inside can be received outside. Signals from outside can't be received inside. The asymmetry is deliberate.

**What Happened Here:**
Nobody built this. The geological survey says it's natural formation. Natural formations don't have right angles. Natural formations don't have doors.

**Active Events:**
- [environmental] frost patterns on the viewport. The patterns are symmetrical. Frost is never symmetrical. (duration: 306 ticks)
- [mammona] HERMES pushed a software update at 0200. The changelog is empty. Colony response time is 0.3 seconds slower. (duration: 553 ticks)
- [social] someone is leaving NutriLoaf wrappers folded into shapes outside specific bunks. The shapes are accurate origami. (duration: 481 ticks)

## LOCATION: Brinemouth
**Planet:** Rhea-2 | **Type:** precursor ruin
**Tone:** corporate_dystopia

**Approach:**
You hear it first. Metal on metal, rhythmic, patient. Something inside is still working.

**Interior:**
The memo was seven pages long. The word 'death' didn't appear. The word 'attrition' appeared nine times.

The air inside is dry. Desiccated. Your lips crack within minutes.

In the storage area: 15 sealed containers. Manifest says 31. The extra containers are not on any record. They are labeled in handwriting, not print.

**History Layers:**
- **Pre-colony (estimated 10,000+ years ago):** Precursor markings on the lower walls. The markings are biological, not carved.
- **First survey (2551):** Survey markers driven into the walls. The markers track a path that leads deeper than the survey authorized.
- **Current posting:** Officially decommissioned. The power draw says otherwise.

**Environmental:**
- Temperature: -19C (hypothermia risk after 15 minutes without thermal gear)
- Atmosphere: breathable but thin (Endurance check every hour, difficulty: normal)
- Radiation: 11 mSv/hr in the lower level (safe for 6 hours, Medical check after)
- Visibility: 61% (the geometry. Corridors that curve when they shouldn't)
- Sound: quiet. The kind of quiet that makes your ears ring. Then you realize the ringing isn't your ears.

**Resources:**
- Thermal cores: extreme, suspiciously so density (Mining check, difficulty: hard)
- Salvage: pre-colony equipment, partially functional (Repair check, difficulty: normal)
- Bioluminescent moss on the walls. The light pattern changes when observed (Survival check, or just pick it. It wants to be found)
- Water: condensation collection possible (Survival check, difficulty: normal)
- Data: terminals still powered. Contents: classified. (Research check, difficulty: hard)

**Territorial:**
- The Ashen Circuit claims: mining rights filed and approved, on paper
- Vanguard Alliance interest: supply cache rumored in lower level
- Ashen Column: believe something important is buried here
- Colony opinion: "the maintenance crew won't go below the second level. Hasn't for weeks."

**Event History:**
- Day 5: Power draw from the site spiked for eleven seconds. The spike doesn't match any equipment on-site.
- Day 33: Two members of a survey team returned. The team had three members.
- Day 112: Three separate colonists reported the same dream involving the site. None had been there.

**Potential Events:**
- If entered after dark: encounter with phenomenon that doesn't appear during day shifts
- If resources taken: the resource regenerates. Faster than it should. Suspiciously.
- If sealed: the seal holds for exactly seventeen days. Then it doesn't.

**Found Here:**
- a pocket watch that runs backward
- a shift schedule with one slot filled in by a hand nobody recognizes
- A data pad: "Someone lived here before us. Not Mammona. Not the survey team. Someone else. The furniture is the wrong shape."

**Location Secrets:**
- Hidden behind a false panel: a communication relay broadcasting on a frequency that predates human radio.
- The colony dogs refuse to enter. Not aggressive refusal. Avoidance. They walk around the perimeter and whine. The path they walk traces a precise circle.
- The structural damage follows a pattern. Not random stress fractures. A sequence. As if something tested each wall in order.

**What Happened Here:**
The previous occupants left in an orderly fashion. Everything labeled, everything stored, everything clean. The only thing they took with them was every mirror in the facility.

**Active Events:**
- [mechanical] the cargo elevator descended to a floor that doesn't exist on the schematics. (duration: 219 ticks)
- [social] a rumor about the supply ship is circulating. The rumor is different depending on who tells it. (duration: 435 ticks)
- [anomalous] a photograph on a colonist's wall has changed. The people in it are in different positions. The colonist hasn't noticed. (duration: 152 ticks)
