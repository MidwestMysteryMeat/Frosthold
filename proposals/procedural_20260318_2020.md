## NPC: Tarn Zhang
**Gender:** Male | **Age:** 25 | **Occupation:** caravan guard
**Traits:** Naturally Immune, Insomniac
**Faction:** Iron Shadow Collective
**Tone:** clinical

**Background:**
Tarn Zhang signed a five-year contract with Solar Nomads. That was nine years ago. He stopped counting when the renewal clause kicked in -- the one buried in paragraph forty-seven that nobody reads.

Other colonists respect Tarn. He does not seem to mind.

He knows about Baldrungen. Not from briefings. From someone who died telling him.

**Dialogue:**
- "They gave us a day off for morale purposes. Nobody knew what to do with it."
- "I am not saying it is alive. I am saying it responds to heat."
- "My kid would be... fifteen now. I think. I've lost track."
- "I used to know every constellation. Here the stars are wrong."
- "Keep your head down. Keep your mouth shut. Keep breathing."
- "I had a name before the contract."
- "I stopped counting days."

**Quest Hook:**
Tarn asks the player to retrieve a Mammona ID badge from Crestfall Colony. Simple job. Except the item is in a room that's been sealed since before the colony arrived.

**Note:** [Tarn should appear in the colony after day 23. Arrives on supply shuttle.]

### [SEQ:2] [Robot/AI] [20:20:10]
============================================================

## UNIT: HULL-04 "Hymn"
**Type:** android | **Station:** Rhea-2
**Tone:** melancholy

**Glitch:** refers to all personnel as 'Dr. Larsen'.

**Background:**
HULL-04 "Hymn" has been operational at Rhea-2 for longer than the colony. Three technicians have been assigned to service it. All three requested transfers within a week.

It has developed a preference for ShockPop Ultra. It does not consume Star Puffs. It arranges the containers.

**Dialogue:**
- "Unit HULL-04 operational. All systems within parameters. Parameters are... flexible."
- "I was built to serve. The question I was not built to ask is: serve what?"
- "I have performed 35841 maintenance cycles. Cycle 35278 was different. I do not have language for how."
- "Please do not touch panel 7. There is nothing behind panel 7. I check every 47 minutes to confirm."
- "If I stop functioning, retrieve the data core. Not for Mammona. For-- I lack the referent. Retrieve it anyway."

**Quest Hook:**
HULL-04 "Hymn" approaches the player with a request it cannot formally make. It has data -- personnel records, manifests, medical files -- from a colony that officially never existed. It wants the player to find out why it has this data. More precisely, it wants the player to find out who it used to be.
