## DATA PAD: Personal Journal -- Caleb Okafor
**Found at:** Surface Rig Nerthus-7 | **Tone:** quiet_terror | **Type:** journal

---

Day 7. Or 8. The clock in the mess is wrong again and nobody fixes it because nobody cares what day it's.

The room was empty. It felt full.

First impressions: it's exactly what the brochure promised if you read between the lines. Cold. Grey. The kind of place where the word 'amenities' means 'there's a roof.' The ShockPop machine in the corridor works, which is more than I expected. Met my bunkmate -- Hyun Altman. They works the drill. Doesn't talk much. Fine by me.

I'm going to keep this journal because I promised myself I'd. A record. Proof I was here. Proof I was me.

---

Day 16.

Getting the rhythm. Shift starts at 0600. NutriLoaf for breakfast. The work is -- work. I'm a permafrost geologist here, same as I was on the last posting. The cold's different though. It gets into the walls, the food, the conversations. Not just temperature. Atmosphere.

Hyun said something strange at dinner: "You'll start hearing things around week three. Everyone does. Don't worry about it." I laughed. They didn't.

---

Day 25.

The colony's dog sat at the airlock and stared. For nine hours.

Okay. So. I'm going to write this down because writing it down makes it real and if it's real then I'm not imagining it and if I'm not imagining it then I need to deal with it.

The new colonist -- arrived last week -- knew my name. Knew my shift. Knew which bunk I sleep in. I've never met their. Nobody introduced us.

I asked Hyun about it. They said: "Yeah." Just that. Yeah. Like I'd finally noticed the weather.

---

Day 47.

Can't sleep. Thought it was the cold but it's not the cold. It's the quiet. Not silence -- there's always noise here, generators, pipes, wind. It's that the quiet is underneath the noise. Like the noise is a blanket over something that's listening.

The footprints led to the wall. Not through it. To it. And stopped.

Hyun hasn't been to their bunk in three nights. They shows up for shift. Eats. Works. But at lights out They goes somewhere else. I followed them last night. Stopped at Section F. The door was open. It shouldn't be open. I didn't go in.

I'm not going to go in.

My hands are shaking as I write this. That's new.

---

Day 76.

I dreamed about home. Except it wasn't home. The proportions were wrong. The sky was the wrong color. My mother's face was my mother's face but the expression belonged to someone who'd studied what 'mother' means without ever having one.

I want to go home. I keep saying that word -- home -- and each time it means less. Like a word you repeat until it's just sounds.

---

Day 82.

I went into Section F last night.

Hyun was there.

Everyone was there.

[The remaining pages contain a single symbol, repeated. It matches no known writing system. Analysis pending. Analysis has been pending for 4 months.]
