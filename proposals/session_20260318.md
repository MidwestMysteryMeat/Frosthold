**NEW CONDITION:** Voidrot

**NAME:** Voidrot

**CAUSE:** Exposure to trace amounts of Voidbloom residue in the atmosphere of certain planets and stations.

**SYMPTOMS (3 Stages):**

1. *Early Stage:*
   - Mild headaches and fatigue
   - Occasional bouts of nausea
   - Slight discoloration of skin around eyes, turning a faint greyish-purple

2. *Mid Stage:*
   - Worsening headaches and fatigue, becoming constant
   - Increased sensitivity to light and loud noises
   - Visible veins beneath skin turning black, starting from the temples and spreading
   - Inability to sleep more than 1-2 hours at a time

3. *Advanced Stage:*
   - Constant, excruciating pain in the skull
   - Hallucinations and episodes of disorientation
   - Complete loss of sight and hearing
   - Veins turn into pulsating black tendrils that spread across skin like vines
   - Death due to internal organ failure caused by Voidrot

**HOW IT SPREADS:** Initially contracted through inhaling trace amounts of Voidbloom, it spreads via contaminated blood transferred through bites or tears from infected colonists. The disease accelerates once exposed to higher concentrations of Voidbloom.

**CURE (if any):** There is no known cure for Voidrot in its advanced stages. Early detection and immediate evacuation from Voidbloom-rich environments can temporarily halt progression, but only complete removal from Voidbloom sources offers permanent relief.
