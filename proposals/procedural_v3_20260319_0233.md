## QUEST: What We Left Behind
**Genre:** Expedition | **Tone:** sudden_dread / melancholy
**Location:** Twin Sun Observatory | **Faction:** Paxtera AgroTech

**Trigger:**
The survey team went out three days ago. They found something in the ruins. Something valuable. Then comms went dead. Lark Abadi picks up a final transmission: coordinates, a single word -- 'hurry' -- and the sound of something large moving through stone.

**Setup:**
Every radio in the colony spoke the same word at the same time. The survey team's last known position is a precursor site twelve kilometers south. The trail is easy to follow -- their vehicle tracks cut clear lines in the ice. Too easy. Halfway there, Lark notices the tracks are paralleled by something else. Footprints. Barefoot. In negative forty. Walking the same direction.

Snow fell through a breach in the ceiling. Nobody fixed it. It was beautiful.

**Objectives:**
1. Reach the survey site. The vehicle is there. Engine cold. Doors open. Equipment scattered. No bodies.
2. Find the team. The footprints -- human and otherwise -- lead into the precursor structure. The structure is larger than the survey map indicated.
3. Retrieve what the team found. It's in the structure. So is whatever took them. Both are between you and the exit.

**The Choice:**
Dynamite the entrance after you're out. Bury whatever's in there. Bury the artifact. Bury the answers.
OR
Leave the entrance open. Study it. Learn from it. Accept that whatever comes out is the price of knowledge.

**Twist:**
The artifact the team found isn't an object. It's information. Carved into the walls: a complete map of every precursor site on Erebus. There are hundreds. And they're connected. Underground. To something central.

**Dialogue During Quest:**
- Lark: "You're the only one I can ask."
- Player: [Respond / Stay Silent / Leave]
- Lark: "I lied on my psych evaluation. They would've flagged me."

**Reward:** 13 thermal cores, a data drive with encrypted coordinates

## QUEST: The Clean Version
**Genre:** Moral Dilemma | **Tone:** shame / defiant
**Location:** Automaton Graveyard | **Faction:** MasTema Incorporated

**Trigger:**
Something happened in the medical wing last night. Something bad. Rin Khoury was there. So were four others. The official report says equipment malfunction. The actual cause: human error. Negligence. One person is dead. If the truth comes out, the colony loses three essential workers to criminal charges. The colony can't afford to lose three people.

**Setup:**
She couldn't meet their eyes. Not since they'd found out. The body has been moved. The scene has been cleaned. The report has been written. Rin stands in the corridor, hands clean, conscience dirty. 'We didn't mean for it to happen,' Rin says. 'But it happened. And telling the truth doesn't bring them back. It just kills three more.'

They said the mine was closed. She went back down anyway.

**Objectives:**
1. Review the falsified report. It's good work. Consistent. Believable. Except for one detail that doesn't add up. You spot it. Others might not.
2. Talk to the four witnesses. They're aligned. Scared. Committed to the lie. But one of them -- the youngest -- keeps looking at the door.
3. Decide. The dead colonist has a family back on Automaton Graveyard. They'll receive a report. This report. The family will never know.

**The Choice:**
Rewrite the report yourself. Not the truth. Not the lie. Something in between. Assign responsibility without criminal charges. A middle ground. Satisfying nobody. Survivable by all.
OR
Confront Rin privately. Make them tell the family. Personally. No report. No charges. Just the truth, delivered to the only people who deserve it.

**Twist:**
The youngest witness breaks. Tells someone. The colony divides. Those who understand and those who don't. The cover-up did more damage than the truth ever would.

**Dialogue During Quest:**
- Rin: "Help me. I can't do this alone."
- Player: [Respond / Stay Silent / Leave]
- Rin: "I did something. On my last posting. I can't undo it."

**Reward:** 11 thermal cores, a weapon that shouldn't exist

## QUEST: The Wrong Face
**Genre:** Survival Horror | **Tone:** dread / noir
**Location:** Novaris-3 / Vanguardus | **Faction:** Vanguard Alliance

**Trigger:**
Two colonists report seeing Cleo Gutierrez in two different sections at the same time. Both are certain. Neither is lying.

**Setup:**
Something scraped against the hull. Rhythmic. Patient. The colony is twenty-three people in a metal box on a frozen rock. Everyone knows everyone. That's what makes it worse -- when Cleo walks into the mess hall, something is different. The laugh is right. The posture is right. The eyes are wrong. Not wrong enough to point at. Wrong enough to feel.

Trust was a commodity. Like rations. You spent it carefully and never got a refund.

**Objectives:**
1. Conduct a headcount. Compare results with HERMES personnel tracking. The numbers don't match.
2. Isolate Cleo and run a medical scan. The results come back normal. Perfectly normal. Suspiciously normal.
3. Find where the real Cleo is. Or determine which one is real. Or accept that neither is.

**The Choice:**
Blood test everyone in the colony. Takes twelve hours. During those twelve hours, nobody sleeps alone. Nobody sleeps at all.
OR
Confront Cleo directly. Force a reaction. Whatever it's, it's been pretending well. Until it stops pretending.

**Twist:**
The medical scans show something. Not in Cleo. In three other colonists. They've been different for weeks.

**Dialogue During Quest:**
- Cleo: "I'm not who they think I am."
- Player: [Respond / Stay Silent / Leave]
- Cleo: "Something's wrong with Section C. Take the long way."

**Reward:** 13 thermal cores, classified intel

## QUEST: Terms Changed
**Genre:** Faction Tension | **Tone:** wrongness / religious_fervor
**Location:** Flooded Wing C | **Faction:** Vanguard Alliance

**Trigger:**
Santiago Petrov has been the player's most reliable ally. Good advice. Solid work. Honest eyes. Until the supply shipment arrives six crates short and Santiago's signature is on the diversion order. The crates went to Vanguard Alliance.

**Setup:**
Snow fell upward near the bore shaft for eleven minutes. Then stopped. You find Santiago in the mess hall. Eating. Calm. They see your face and they know that you know. They don't run. They set down their spoon. 'I can explain,' they say. 'You won't like it. But I can explain.'

He preached to the shift change. Three converts by Thursday. The colony wasn't sure what they'd converted to.

**Objectives:**
1. Listen to Santiago's explanation. Decide if it changes anything. The crates are gone either way.
2. Determine what was in the diverted crates. Standard supplies, according to the manifest. But the manifest was falsified. The real contents: a Black Maw tribute token -- safe passage, one use. Six crates of it.
3. Confront Vanguard Alliance's contact. They're still on the colony. Waiting for the second shipment. The one Santiago hasn't diverted yet.

**The Choice:**
Take the second shipment and use it as leverage against Vanguard Alliance. You've what they want. Make them pay.
OR
Return it. All of it. No debts. No leverage. No entanglement. Clean books. Cold comfort.

**Twist:**
Santiago's explanation is simple: the colony is going to fail. They saw the numbers. Made a deal to get people out. Not everyone. But some. They chose who gets saved. Your name isn't on the list.

**Dialogue During Quest:**
- Santiago: "Don't leave me here. Not in the dark."
- Player: [Respond / Stay Silent / Leave]
- Santiago: "The perimeter lights keep cutting out. Same section. Every night."

**Reward:** 10 thermal cores, a debt erased

## QUEST: Paragraph Twelve
**Genre:** Moral Dilemma | **Tone:** mania / furious
**Location:** The Gutter's Pearl | **Faction:** Iron Shadow Collective

**Trigger:**
Rowan da Silva finds a file in Iron Shadow Collective's local database. It was supposed to be encrypted. It's not. Inside: records of seven colonies. Seven failures. Not accidents. Designed failures. Insurance payouts. Tax write-offs. The colony they're standing in is number eight.

**Setup:**
She hadn't slept in four days and the ideas wouldn't stop. Pages of diagrams. Brilliant. Illegible. Rowan has the file on a data drive. Small enough to hide. Large enough to end Iron Shadow Collective. Or large enough to end Rowan. The file includes projected casualty timelines. For this colony. The timeline is ahead of schedule.

The safety equipment hadn't been inspected in eight months. The reports said otherwise.

**Objectives:**
1. Verify the file. Cross-reference the seven colonies with public records. The losses match. The insurance claims match. The 'unforeseen circumstances' are identical.
2. Decide what to do with the evidence. The comms array can broadcast to the wider network. Iron Shadow Collective can't stop a broadband pulse. But they can trace it.
3. Protect Rowan. Once this file moves, Rowan becomes a target. Maybe they already are.

**The Choice:**
Broadcast the file. Burn Iron Shadow Collective. Save future colonies. Paint a target on this one. Iron Shadow Collective will retaliate. Not with soldiers. With supply cuts. With silence. The cold will do the rest.
OR
Bury it. Use it as leverage. Quiet negotiations. Better conditions. The truth stays buried. But so does this colony's death sentence.

**Twist:**
The casualty timeline for this colony has a footnote: 'Exception: if thermal core yield exceeds projections, initiate Protocol 7.' The yield exceeds projections. Protocol 7 isn't a shutdown. It's an acceleration.

**Dialogue During Quest:**
- Rowan: "Something's changed. I can't tell you what. But something's changed."
- Player: [Respond / Stay Silent / Leave]
- Rowan: "Don't go near the bore shaft after dark."

**Reward:** 5 thermal cores, a weapon that shouldn't exist
