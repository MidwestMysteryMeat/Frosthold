## QUEST: The Empty Bunk
**Tone:** furious | **Location:** Orbit Hub 71 | **Faction:** Black Maw

**Trigger:**
A data pad appears in the player's quarters. No sender. It contains coordinates and a single word: 'come.'

**Setup:**
The air in Orbit Hub 71 tastes like copper. The lights work but they're the wrong color -- shifted toward a spectrum that makes everything look like it is underwater. Minho Ironmere leads the player to a room at the end of the corridor. The door is open. It should not be. The room contains a window. There should not be a window here. Through the window: a landscape that does not match the planet they are on..

**Objectives:**
1. Follow Minho Ironmere to the site. Observe. Do not touch anything yet.
2. Access the terminal. The password is written on the wall in something that is not paint.
3. Return to colony. Decide who to tell. Decide who to trust. They are not the same list.

**The Choice:**
**Choice A:** Give the evidence to Black Maw. They'll bury it. Minho Ironmere survives. The truth does not.
**Choice B:** Broadcast it. Everyone hears. Minho Ironmere is gone within a day. The colony changes forever.

**Twist:**
Minho Ironmere is not who they said they were. Their real name is on one of the documents. Under the heading 'Subjects.'

**Dialogue During Quest:**
- Minho Ironmere: "I've been sitting on this for weeks. Couldn't sleep. Couldn't eat. Couldn't tell anyone who works for them."
- Player: [Respond / Stay Silent / Leave]
- Minho Ironmere: "I know you do not trust me. That's fine. Trust what you see."

**Reward:** 11 thermal cores, a weapon that should not exist
